## Why the engines differ

- **Varna** (1,458 pairs). PyJHora assigns Taurus, Virgo and Capricorn to Shudra and Gemini, Libra and Aquarius to Vaishya. North Indian almanacs and onlinejyotish (Muhurta Chintamani) have it the other way round, as we do. Example: boy Krittika pada 2, girl Mrigashira pada 3: Likha 1, PyJHora 0.
- **Vashya** (6,153 pairs). PyJHora splits Sagittarius and Capricorn by pada number instead of at 15 degrees (its own code notes this is an approximation), and uses the saravali.de table. Ours takes the majority cell of three published tables and the classical 15 degree split. Example: boy Ashwini pada 1, girl Mrigashira pada 3: Likha 1, PyJHora 0.5.
- **Tara** (3,888 pairs). PyJHora's North Tara credits only Kshema, Sadhaka and Mitra (4th, 6th, 8th) and gives nothing for Janma, Sampat and Ati-Mitra (1st, 2nd, 9th), which the classical rule counts as favourable. So the same nakshatra scores 0 there and 3 here. Our Tara matches the classical rule on all 11,664 pairs. Example: boy Ashwini pada 1, girl Ashwini pada 1: Likha 3, PyJHora 0.
- **Bhakoot** (81 pairs). PyJHora's Bhakoot table scores 7 for one direction of one 6/8 pair (girl Aquarius, boy Cancer) and 0 for every other 6/8 in both directions. That single cell looks like a typo; we score all 6/8 the same. Example: boy Punarvasu pada 4, girl Dhanishta pada 3: Likha 0, PyJHora 7.

Yoni, Graha Maitri, Gana and Nadi agree on every pair. Rules and sources for every table: `likha_kundli/rules.py`.
